PROJECT ORIGINS

About WildWiki

WildWiki started as a mini-wiki project focused on wildlife preservation and education. By combining clean structure with modern design, we aim to showcase the beauty of the animal kingdom.

Why Just HTML & CSS?

This version of the project strips away complex logic to focus on the fundamentals of web development. Everything you see here—from the mobile menu to the image carousels—is built natively without JavaScript (save for a simple dark mode toggle!).
